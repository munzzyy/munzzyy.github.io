  • 2026-09-30
    The five-minute freeze

    Location sharing kept stopping on locked Pixels, and every test I ran said it was fixed. None of those tests ran past five minutes, which is when Chromium freezes a hidden page.

  • 2026-09-30
    A relay that doesn't need Cloudflare

    Starling's listing said self-hostable. Someone pointed out that meant self-hostable on Cloudflare, and that the listing never said who hosted the default relay. Both got fixed.

  • 2026-09-30
    A second place your location goes

    Someone wanted Starling to also feed their own Reitti and Home Assistant. Sending the data was easy. Making sure the feature can't be turned against the person holding the phone took the thought.

  • 2026-09-30
    Three apps under the clock

    A Pixel Fold's outer screen showed Sepia's top bar behind the clock and the camera. Android 15 made every app edge to edge, and three of mine had never noticed.

  • 2026-08-29
    SPI with no wire in one direction

    A U-Boot binding allowed a bus width of zero and nothing downstream understood what that meant. Two patches later, mainline does.

  • 2026-08-29
    An overread GNU cpio didn't have (yet)

    A patch got rejected on a correct POSIX argument, which turned out not to be the argument that mattered. Reframing it around malformed input is what got it fixed.

  • 2026-08-10
    U-Boot never had a btrfs test

    Writing the filesystem tests U-Boot was missing: a reproducer that had to be synthesized byte by byte, a regression the suite caught, and three patches now in mainline.

  • 2026-08-10
    Two kernel patches for the Flipper One

    A devicetree VBUS fix where my first read was wrong, and a dwc3 gadget fix whose answer had been sitting on the mailing list since 2022.
